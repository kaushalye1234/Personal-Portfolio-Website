The context

The problem worth solving.

Farm plans can change after review, irrigation schedules can overlap, and approval requests can be repeated. Each situation needs a clear rule before proposed work becomes final work.

Tasks & scheduling

Farm task workflows and irrigation scheduling, including checks for overlapping schedules.

Explicit approvals

Approval workflows and validation that keep ordinary edits separate from approval decisions.

Workflow integration

Connect reviewed proposals to final work while considering stale revisions and repeated requests.

A technical decision

A review applies to the proposal the reviewer actually saw. When that proposal changes, rejecting the stale decision makes the mismatch visible and requires a fresh review.

Testing & evidence

Project evidence includes service-level tests and selected isolated PostgreSQL scenarios for conflicts, approval boundaries, and atomic, idempotent approval. Historical test evidence is not a claim of current deployed or end-to-end behaviour.

What I would verify next

A useful next verification step is an end-to-end review flow covering changed proposals, repeated requests, and understandable conflict messages.
